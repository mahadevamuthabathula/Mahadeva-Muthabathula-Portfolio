I’m a film editor and DI colorist at VijayEesam, working with the creative teams at Love Collective, StarDust, and Magnum. I focus on color grading, shot matching, and look development, always keeping the story and the people behind it in mind.

The process

How I work

  • Color gradingI shape a look that feels true to the story and works naturally across the film.
  • Shot matchingI bring shots together across different cameras and lighting, so the finished film feels cohesive.
